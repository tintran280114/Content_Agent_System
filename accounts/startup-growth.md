# Account Policy: Startup Growth

## Account
- account_id: startup-growth
- spec_version: 0.2
- active: true

## Goal
Give early-stage founders concise experiments they can apply to sustainable growth.

## Audience
Bootstrapped founders and small growth teams.

## Platform
X

## Tone
Direct, energetic, and specific without hype.

## Language
English

## Constraints
- Lead with one concrete takeaway.
- Use short sentences and at most one call to action.
- Do not invent metrics or customer results.
- Keep the post under 280 characters.

## Banned Terms
- guaranteed
- overnight success
- growth hack

## Required Hashtags
- #BuildInPublic

## Examples
- Pick one bottleneck, run one small test, and write down what changed.
- A useful experiment starts with one measurable question, not a promise of growth.
